Business Messaging

SMS, WhatsApp, RCS, voice, email and Instagram on one platform, with secure login, chatbots and automation built in.

One platform for every customer conversation

Send updates, run campaigns, verify users and answer customers on every channel, from one panel or one API.

D-TECH sets up and supports business messaging for your company: official WhatsApp Business messaging, bulk and transactional SMS, RCS, voice calls and IVR, email and Instagram. Chatbots answer round the clock, a shared inbox lets your team take over, and a message that is not delivered or read is sent again on another channel. It connects to your CRM, ERP or online store so messages go out on their own.

Every channel your customers use

Use one channel or all of them. Each can send updates, run campaigns and handle replies.

SMS

Fast, reliable texts to any mobile phone.

  • OTP, alerts and transactional SMS
  • Bulk promotional campaigns
  • Regional languages and Unicode
  • DLT templates imported in one step
  • Automatic short links with click tracking

WhatsApp Business

The official WhatsApp Business API, on the app customers open most.

  • Business account and template approvals handled
  • Broadcasts with images, documents and buttons
  • Scheduled and retargeting campaigns
  • Order, cart and payment updates from your store
  • Several agents chatting on one number

RCS messaging

Rich, branded messages in the phone’s own messaging app.

  • Verified sender name and logo
  • Images, videos and product carousels
  • Action buttons, quick replies and payment links
  • Read receipts and two-way chat
  • Sent as SMS where RCS is not available

Voice

Automated calls for when a text is not enough.

  • Voice broadcasts with recorded messages
  • Press 1 to show interest or reach an agent
  • Missed-call numbers for enquiries
  • Cloud IVR and call centre
  • Call recording, logs and reports

Email

Bulk and transactional email that reaches the inbox.

  • Drag-and-drop builder and ready templates
  • Order, invoice and account emails by API or SMTP
  • Signup forms with double opt-in
  • Segments, personalisation and automation
  • Sender authentication (SPF, DKIM, DMARC) set up

Instagram messaging

Answer followers without leaving your inbox.

  • Direct messages in the shared inbox
  • Quick replies and saved responses
  • Chats assigned to the right person
  • Hand-over from chatbot to your team

Secure login and verification

Verify users quickly and stop fraud logins, with OTPs or without them.

OTP API

Generate and check one-time passwords with two simple API calls.

  • Set code length, expiry and retries
  • Built-in limits to stop misuse
  • Delivery by SMS, WhatsApp or email

Automatic OTP backup

If an OTP SMS is late, the same code goes out another way.

  • A voice call reads the code out
  • Email or WhatsApp as a further backup
  • Fewer failed logins and sign-ups

OTP-free login

The user sends a code instead of waiting for one.

  • One tap opens WhatsApp or SMS with the code filled in
  • Checked against the number, website and IP address
  • No code to intercept, no SMS to pay for

Silent SIM verification

The mobile number is confirmed through the mobile network, in the background.

  • Nothing for the user to type
  • Cannot be phished or shared
  • Works on any phone, no app needed

Fraud checks

Extra signals before you let a user in or approve a payment.

  • Recent SIM-swap detection
  • IP and location matching
  • Blocks emulators and bots

Encrypted SMS

Sensitive text such as OTPs and bank details is encrypted.

  • Hidden from everyone, support staff included
  • Available through the SMS API
  • Suited to banking, finance and insurance

Tools that do the work for you

Chatbots, a team inbox and automatic flows across every channel.

Shared team inbox

WhatsApp, RCS, Instagram and Facebook chats in one place.

  • Automatic or manual chat assignment
  • Agent shift timings and off-hours replies
  • Escalation when an agent does not reply
  • Tag contacts as hot lead, cold lead or DND

No-code chatbots

Build WhatsApp and RCS chatbots without writing code.

  • Drag-and-drop flow builder
  • Start a flow from keywords
  • Hand over to an agent at any step
  • Live data from your systems through webhooks

AI answers from your documents

Upload a brochure, manual or FAQ, and the bot answers customer questions from it.

  • PDF and Word documents
  • On your website chat and on WhatsApp
  • Answers day and night

Channel fallback flows

A message that does not land is sent again on the next channel.

  • Branch on not delivered, delivered, read or clicked
  • For example WhatsApp, then SMS, then email
  • Your own API can choose the next step

Campaign manager

Plan, send and measure campaigns from one screen.

  • Import contacts from Excel or CSV
  • Segments by location, interest or purchase
  • Personalised messages and scheduled sends
  • Automatic resend to those who missed it

Integrations

Messages go out from the software you already use.

  • CRM and ERP, including Odoo
  • Shopify, WooCommerce and other stores
  • Payment links and order events
  • Webhooks and Zapier

Voice and call centre

Reach people by phone and handle incoming calls from the cloud, with no hardware to install.

Voice broadcast

Recorded messages to large lists at once.

  • Your own number or a custom caller ID
  • Press 1 for interest or to reach an agent
  • Resend to unanswered and busy numbers
  • Duplicate and invalid numbers removed on import

Missed-call service

Customers give a missed call; you get a lead.

  • Print the number on ads, packs and SMS
  • Caller saved to your list at once
  • Automatic thank-you SMS
  • Email alert so your team can call back

Cloud IVR and call centre

A full call centre that runs in the cloud.

  • Multi-level IVR and custom call flows
  • Skills-based routing and auto dialer
  • Click-to-call, voicemail and conference calls
  • Recording, live monitoring and call logs

Reports and developer tools

Use the web panel, or connect your own app, website or software through the API.

Live delivery, read and click reports
REST and SMPP APIs with webhooks
Short links that track clicks and devices
Share PDFs, images and videos by short link
Regional languages on every channel
Surveys, feedback forms and offer codes
