    Questions attendees ask

    Do I need an account?

    No. You only need a working email address and a mobile number.

    When do I get the meeting link or venue details?

    Joining details for online events are in your ticket email, sent once your registration is confirmed. They are not shown publicly.

    I paid but have no ticket yet.

    Paid registrations are verified by hand against our bank records, usually within two working days. Write to sales@dtechindia.com with your reference and we will check.

    What happens to my details?

    They are used only to run the event and send your ticket, and are kept in D-TECH’s private records. See our Privacy Policy.
