Automated Tanker Fleet Management System

Real-time tracking, optimised operations, regulatory compliance, cost savings and enhanced safety, delivered with efficiency and reliability.

Smart · Automated · Always reliable

Manless tanker management for terminals and depots

Our automated (manless) Tanker Fleet Management System is a complete digital solution for managing tanker movement within terminals, depots and industrial facilities.

By eliminating manual processes, it delivers faster turnaround, improved transparency and smooth traffic flow, without human intervention. Built with industrial-grade, weather-proof hardware, the system runs reliably 24×7, even in rain, dust and harsh outdoor environments.

How it works

A weather-proof system built for uninterrupted operations

  1. Digital token issuanceTokens are issued digitally, with real-time queue control for organised tanker movement.
  2. Live LED display boardsLED boards show live tanker status and token numbers.
  3. Built for tough conditionsRugged, weather-resistant hardware designed for uninterrupted 24×7 operation and continuous fleet performance.
  4. Accuracy and access controlMinimises human error, prevents unauthorised access and ensures operational accuracy.
