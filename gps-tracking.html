GPS Vehicle Tracking & Fleet Telematics

Real-time visibility of every vehicle, consignment and route.

Every vehicle on one live screen

Know where each vehicle is, how it is being driven and what it is costing you, from a single dashboard on your desk or phone.

D-TECH supplies and fits GPS trackers and sensors in your trucks, tankers, vans and buses, sets up the tracking platform with your users and alerts, and stays on to support it. Live data can also flow into your ERP or your own applications.

What you can see and control

Location is only the start. The same device and sensors tell you about fuel, temperature, driving and time on the road.

Live location with trip replay
Fuel level monitoring and theft alerts
Temperature monitoring for cold-chain cargo
Geofence, overspeed and ignition alerts
Trip, idle-time and driver reports
API link to your ERP or apps

What you get

  1. GPS / GPRS tracking devicesFitted and wired into each vehicle.
  2. Fuel and temperature sensorsAdded where the vehicle or cargo needs them.
  3. Vehicle data integrationEngine and CAN data read where the vehicle supports it.
  4. Web dashboard and mobile appFor managers, dispatchers and owners, with role-based logins.
  5. Alerts and scheduled reportsSet to the rules that matter to your operation.
