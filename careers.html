A bridge between business needs and affordable, high-quality IT

D-TECH Solution Integrators connects what businesses need with dependable, affordable IT: servers, workstations, storage, desktops, laptops, networks, automation and the people who keep them running.

Mr. Bhaavik Barot started the business in 1999 with commercial sales of servers, workstations, storage, desktops and laptops.
